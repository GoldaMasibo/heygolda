01 About

Hello.

I am a People and Culture professional at heart who happens to spend her days recruiting.

I like the whole journey a person takes into a workplace: the job ad, the first conversation, the interview, the offer, and the part nobody talks about, which is how it all felt. I am happiest when a messy process turns into a clear one, and when I catch myself doing something by hand for the third time, I write a script.

Outside work I read, walk, lift, and nag my friends to move to my side of town so we can do all three together. I also help with a small online accessories and footwear business that belongs to my sister.

02 Work

What I am proud of

A few searches and systems, in my own words.

  • 1

    The search nobody could close

    I took over a director search that had stalled for months, rebuilt how we found people, brought in a partner to assess the shortlist, and filled it with someone I had sourced myself.

  • 2

    A director, placed in a month

    I filled a senior analytics and planning director role within a month, and the role was later grown into a vice president position.

  • 3

    Hiring that runs itself, a little

    I built scheduling that lets candidates book their own interviews and timed assignments that go out on their own, so my time goes to people instead of calendars.

  • 4

    Hard briefs, honest conversations

    On-site only roles, local-only hiring, tight budgets. I like the searches where you have to be creative and honest with everyone at once.

09 Notes

For job seekers

A few years of hiring, and a lot of CVs.

  1. Do not vibe apply

    Skip roles that are not a good fit for you. Your time is better spent on fewer, better applications.

  2. One role per company, not ten

    You may love what they love, but applying for a finance internship and a VP of People and Culture in the same week is a red flag.

  3. People are biased, so make it work for you

    A clean, readable CV, preferably a PDF. Show up to interviews looking like you like yourself: good internet if virtual, a put-together look, clear speech. And for goodness' sake, be brief.
